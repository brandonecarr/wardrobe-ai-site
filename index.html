Wardrobe AI

Wardrobe AI catalogues the clothes you own and dresses you from them: one outfit each morning, with a reason for every piece, that learns from what you wear, swap and skip.

Support

Email brandonecarr@gmail.com. Inside the app, Profile → "Talk to a person" opens the same email with your account id filled in, which is the fastest way to get help with your own data.

Your data

Everything the app stores is private to your account. Profile → Account lets you export every record as a file or delete the account and all of its data in one tap. Details are in the privacy policy.

Subscriptions

Premium is billed by Apple. Change or cancel it in your Apple Account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions); nothing changes from inside the app.
